Policy

Privacy

This page describes the marketing site (HTML in website/) and high-level notes about the desktop app. It is not a substitute for your own compliance review.

This website

These pages are static files. We do not run first-party analytics, advertising trackers, or mandatory cookies in the shipped HTML. When you open a page from disk (file://), no web server logs are generated. When you host the folder on your own server, your hosting provider may log requests as usual.

Links to GitHub (downloads, source, licensing inquiries) are subject to GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts may load from Google Fonts via the stylesheet in site.css; see Google’s policy if that applies to your deployment.

The desktop application

Chat transcripts, knowledge base content, wiki, download metadata, and metrics samples are stored in a local SQLite database in the app’s user data area on your machine. Model weights and converted files live in directories you configure. See Documentation → Privacy for product behavior.

Hugging Face receives network requests when you search or download models in the app, under your control. Ollama and llama.cpp are separate runtimes with their own policies and update channels.

Commercial inquiries

If you open a GitHub issue for licensing, the content you submit is visible according to that repository’s settings (public by default). Do not include secrets or personal data you are not comfortable sharing.

Changes

This text may be updated when the site adds analytics, embedded forms, or other data collection. The website/ folder in the repository is the source of truth.
