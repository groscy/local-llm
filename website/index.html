Capabilities

Built for serious local workflows

Everything runs on your hardware. Swap backends, attach knowledge, and keep telemetry optional.

◇

Dual runtime

Use Ollama for fast registry pulls or llama.cpp with your own .gguf files—selected from the same top bar.

◎

Hugging Face hub

Search and download models from the Hub into your configured folder, with progress and resume-friendly transfers.

✦

RAG & wiki

Ingest sources, compile markdown wiki pages, search chunks, and highlight terms inline in chat replies.

⌁

Training hook

Optional local LoRA training pipeline for experimentation—wired as a separate step so inference stays snappy. Metrics and pinned widgets keep GPU and token stats visible when you want them.
