Local LLM Desktop is offered under a simple tiered model: no fee for personal and academic use; commercial and enterprise use requires a paid license from the licensor.

Summary

  • Academic — Free for accredited schools, faculty, students, and non-sponsored research.
  • Enterprise / commercial — Requires a purchased license for business use, internal tools used primarily for commercial purposes, or inclusion in paid offerings.
